My Interests:

Mountain Biking:

I have been mountain biking for about 10 years now and it is one of my favourite activities. I often ride at hartland in Victoria, but also spending time at whistler challenging myself on the much larger features that whistler offers. As an added bonus, it has been fantastic at keeping me feeling healthy.

Diving:

I have been Diving for the last few years now and it is a great way for me to relax. There are plenty of spots on Vancouver Island that are great for diving. I look forward to going and seeing more spots this summer!

Cooking:

Prior to going to university, I cooked in different retaurants over a 10 year period. After exiting the industry, I began to fall in love with cooking again. It has been a great way for me to relax and be creative.

Traveling:

Every summer I try to go on a trip somehwere. In the past two years I went to Peru and hiked macchu picchu, and went to Vietnam and explored the country. I have currently begun the booking process for a trip planned to Japan. Japanese cuisine is one of my favourite and I am looking forward to trying it.
